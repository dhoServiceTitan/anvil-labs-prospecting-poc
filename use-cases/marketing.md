# Use Case: Marketing

## Task
Help marketing managers track campaign performance, lead generation
metrics, and channel attribution across active programs.

## Entities
- Campaign: name, channel, status, budget, spend, leads_generated, cpl
- LeadSource: channel, leads, conversion_rate, revenue_attributed
- ContentPiece: title, type, publish_date, views, leads, conversion_rate

## Sample Queries
- Which campaigns have the lowest cost per lead this month?
- Show me content pieces driving the most pipeline
- Which channels have the best lead-to-opportunity conversion rate?

## User Journey
1. Manager asks about campaign or channel performance
2. Results appear in the panel — each row shows campaign or channel data
   with budget health badges and trend indicators
3. Manager can click a campaign to see daily breakdown
4. Clicking a content piece shows associated campaigns
